    HEN I first went to Russia I had a trans-lator for a while whom I used to call Three-in-One, though she never knew why. Everything inRussia was always dandy for Galya, but if suddenchanges were made they were dandy too. She sawno evil, spoke no evil, heard no evil. She wasmonotonous company but an excellent translator.One day I played rather an unkind trick on her.Late the night before, the Foreign Office hadissued a special press communique announcing thatthe title Marshal of the Soviet Union had beenconferred on Comrade Stalin. We were going tovisit a school early in the morning and when westarted out I knew that Three-in-One hadn’t seenthe papers, which didn’t appear till around ten. Iasked her how it was that Stalin could be supremec-in-c and head of the defense council, when hewasn’t a marshal, nor even a general.
